Talkivo documentation

How to use Talkivo, step by step. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Talkivo lets you talk to strangers from different locations and countries through live audio, video and text chat — one-on-one, or in a group of up to four. It was built by Miyoni.

Getting started

  1. Open talkivo.de5.net in your browser. You do not need an account to try it.
  2. Choose how you want to talk: Text, Voice or Video.
  3. Choose One-on-one or Group (up to 4 people).
  4. Optionally type a nickname, pick your country, and add interests. People who share an interest with you are matched first.
  5. Tick the box to confirm you are 18 or older, then press Start (or Find a group).
  6. For voice or video your browser asks to use your microphone and camera. Press Allow.
  7. When someone is found, the chat opens. Press Next to leave and meet someone new, or leave the page to stop.

Text, voice and video

  • Text: type a message and press Send. You see when the other person is typing.
  • Voice: a live audio call. Use the microphone button to mute yourself.
  • Video: a live audio and video call. You can also turn your camera off.

You are only matched with people who chose the same way of talking as you. Text, voice and video rooms are separate.

One-on-one or group

  • One-on-one: you and one other person. When either of you leaves, the chat ends.
  • Group: a room of up to 4 people (you included). The room stays open while people join and leave, and it never takes a fifth person. If you are the only one left, you can wait for others or press Next to join a different group.
  • In a group you see each person's name next to their messages, and a note when someone joins or leaves. People who join late do not see earlier messages.

Playing music in a call

During a voice or video call you can share music with everyone in the call:

  1. Press the music button in the call.
  2. Add songs from your device. They play from your phone or computer; they are not uploaded to our servers.
  3. Use play, pause, next, previous, repeat and the volume slider. Everyone in the call hears the music together with your voice.

Muting your microphone does not stop the music. Only play music you have the right to share.

A countdown ("Time left") shows during a chat. Time only counts while you are in a chat. When it runs out, the chat ends and a message tells you what to do next.

Create an account or sign in

  1. Press Sign in (or Create a free account when your time is up).
  2. Type your email address and press Email me a code.
  3. Open the email and type the 6-digit code. If this is your first time, your account is created; otherwise you are signed in.

Did not get the email? Check your spam folder, wait a minute, and press Send again. Codes can be requested only a few times per hour.

Guest time is counted on the device you use. Signing in gives you the account allowance on any device.

The Android app

On Android you may see a message offering the Talkivo app. Press Install app and the download starts at once. Open the downloaded file to install it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser, because the app is not on Google Play. Press Not now to hide the message.

Light and dark

Use the moon or sun button on any screen to switch between light and dark. Light is the default and your choice is remembered on that device.

Staying safe

  • Talkivo is for adults (18+). Do not use it if you are younger.
  • Do not share your full name, address, phone number, passwords, bank details or any private information.
  • Strangers can say anything. If someone makes you uncomfortable, press Next straight away.
  • In voice and video calls, the people you are connected to can see your network (IP) address. This is how direct calls work.
  • We do not record calls. Other people could, so only share what you are comfortable with.
  • To report abuse, email prof200619@gmail.com.

See also the Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions.

Troubleshooting

The camera or microphone does not work

Check that you pressed Allow when the browser asked, and that no other app is using them. In your browser's site settings for Talkivo, set Camera and Microphone to Allow, then reload. Talkivo only works on secure (https) addresses.

I connect but cannot hear or see the other person

Calls go directly between devices. Some strict mobile or office networks block direct connections. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Text chat works even when calls cannot connect.

"Time's up" appeared and I have not chatted much

Your chat time counts for as long as you are in a chat, including while you wait quietly. Guests have 10 minutes in total; accounts get 30 minutes that refresh after 2 hours.

I did not receive the sign-in code

Check spam or junk, make sure the address is typed correctly, wait a minute, and press Send again. If it still does not arrive, email prof200619@gmail.com.

Nobody is found

Matching needs other people searching at the same time for the same mode. Try again in a moment, or try text mode, which usually has the most people.

Questions and answers

Is Talkivo free?

Yes. There is a free allowance of chat time for guests and for accounts.

Do you keep my messages?

No chat history is kept. Text messages pass through our server only to reach the other people in the chat and are discarded shortly after the chat ends. Voice, video and music go directly between devices. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Email prof200619@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. Talkivo runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

How many people can be in a group?

Up to four, including you.
