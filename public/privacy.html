Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information Talkivo collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Talkivo is built and run by Miyoni ("we", "us"). You can reach us at prof200619@gmail.com.

In short: we ask for as little as possible, we do not keep chat history, we do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

1. What we collect

If you create an account

  • Your email address.
  • When your account was created, when you last signed in, and how many times you have signed in.
  • How much of your chat time you have used (so we can apply the time limit and its refresh).
  • A sign-in session. A random code is stored in your browser; we keep only a scrambled (hashed) version on our side. Sessions last up to 30 days or until you sign out.

If you use Talkivo without an account (guest)

  • A random device ID that is generated in your browser. We use it only to count your guest chat time. It is not linked to your name or email.

When you search for a chat

  • The details you enter or choose: nickname, country, interests and the type of chat you want (text, voice, video, one-on-one or group). These are sent to our server to find you a match, and are shown to the people you are matched with.

During a chat

  • Text messages pass through our server so they can reach the other people in the chat. They are held only in the server's memory while the chat is active and for a couple of minutes after it ends, and are then discarded. We do not write them to a database and we keep no chat history.
  • Voice, video and music travel directly between the devices in the call (peer to peer). They do not pass through our servers and we do not record them. Songs you play stay on your device.
  • To set up a call, short technical connection messages are relayed through our server for a few minutes.

Technical information

  • Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address and request details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We briefly use network addresses in memory to limit abuse, such as too many sign-in code requests. We do not store IP addresses in our account records.

2. Information stored in your browser

Talkivo does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for these things only:

WhatWhy
Guest device IDTo count guest chat time
Sign-in sessionTo keep you signed in
Saved nickname, country and interestsSo you do not have to type them again
18+ confirmationSo we do not ask every time
Light/dark choiceTo remember your theme
App message choiceSo the Android app message stops once you dismiss it or install
Temporary chat identity (cleared when you close the tab)To keep your place in a chat

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. Clearing your guest device ID gives you a new guest ID.

3. How we use information

  • To match you with other people and run chats, calls and groups.
  • To create your account, send your sign-in code, and keep you signed in.
  • To apply chat-time limits.
  • To keep Talkivo secure and prevent spam, abuse and misuse.
  • To let the site owner see the list of registered accounts (email address, sign-up date, last sign-in, sign-in count and chat time used) in an admin dashboard that only approved administrators can open.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.

4. Who else handles information

We do not sell your information. These services help us run Talkivo:

  • Cloudflare — hosts Talkivo, delivers pages, and stores account records and chat-time records in its storage. Your data may be processed in different countries on its network.
  • Smartbase — sends the sign-in code emails and checks the code you type. It receives your email address for this purpose.
  • Google and Cloudflare connection servers (STUN) — help devices find each other for calls. They can see your IP address when your device contacts them.
  • Google Fonts — the web page loads its typeface from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm or to protect Talkivo.

5. Other people in a chat

  • Other participants see the nickname, country and interests you choose, and anything you say or show.
  • In voice and video calls, the people you are connected to can see your IP address. This is how direct calls work.
  • We cannot stop another person from recording, copying or sharing what you say or show. Please do not share personal or private information with strangers.

6. How long we keep information

  • Text messages: in memory only, discarded shortly after the chat ends.
  • Account information: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Chat-time records: as long as needed to apply the time limits. Guest limits are meant not to refresh, so guest records are kept.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can use Talkivo without an account.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about your account by emailing prof200619@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. Contact us and we will respond.

8. Children

Talkivo is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not meant for children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using Talkivo, email us and we will act on it.

9. Security

Talkivo is served over HTTPS. Sign-in sessions are stored in hashed form on our side. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use Talkivo after a change means you accept the updated policy.
